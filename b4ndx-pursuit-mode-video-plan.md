# B4NDX Pursuit Mode — Hero Video Blueprint

## Global definitions

| Field | Specification |
| --- | --- |
| Purpose | Homepage hero for the B4NDX Discord bot website; establish high-performance control, precision, and an automotive signature. |
| Audience | Discord server owners, admins, and community operators who want an advanced but calm automation system. |
| Duration | 6 seconds, designed to loop from a steady final state back to a dark resting frame. |
| Aspect ratio | 16:9 landscape; the website will crop safely for mobile. |
| Visual style | Luxury automotive campaign realism; black Mercedes performance coupe, dark underground concrete architecture, low-key graphite lighting, wet floor reflections, restricted Signal Lime accents. |
| Color and lighting | Midnight Black, graphite, metallic silver, focused cool-white headlights, a single Signal Lime scan/reflection. No broad neon wash. |
| Recurring object | A glossy black modern Mercedes performance coupe, three-quarter front angle, silver grille star, low stance, visible only in the dark architectural bay. |
| Dialogue and narration | None. |
| Audio | Embedded sound design only: distant low engine-idle rumble, short electrical ignition pulse, restrained low-frequency cinematic impact on full headlight activation. No background music. |

## Clip plan

| Field | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| Narrative purpose | Product hero / establish the B4NDX visual signature. |
| Pacing | Slow and deliberate. |
| Scene | Dark underground architectural parking bay with wet charcoal concrete, a glossy black Mercedes already parked facing three-quarter toward camera. |
| Content action | A thin Signal Lime scan travels across the grille; the left headlight flickers, then both headlights power on and cast a controlled white-lime beam across the wet floor. The camera pushes forward slightly, then holds. |
| Transition description | The glossy black Mercedes performance coupe is already present in the dark parking bay at the start, with its body, silver grille star, and wet floor reflections visible only as restrained silhouettes. A thin Signal Lime scanning light moves from left to right across the grille and body panels without changing the car’s position; as it reaches the headlight, the left lamp gives one quick dangerous flicker, then both cool-white headlights ignite together. The beams remain on throughout the final seconds, their white-lime reflections stretching forward across the wet concrete as the camera makes a very slow forward push and ends on a stable, loop-friendly composition. |
| Target duration | 6 seconds. |
| Camera movement | Very slow, controlled dolly forward. |
| First keyframe framing | Wide 16:9 low-angle front three-quarter automobile shot; car placed on the right, generous dark negative space on the left for website typography. |
| Sound effects | Engine-idle low rumble throughout; short electrical charge beginning near the scan; crisp headlight activation click and a restrained sub-bass impact at ignition. |
| Background music | None. |

## Mandatory visual reference

One 16:9 primary reference image is required: black Mercedes performance coupe in the same dark underground architecture, headlights still off, car on the right, and clean negative space left. The visual reference must have no text, logos, labels, annotations, or watermarks.
